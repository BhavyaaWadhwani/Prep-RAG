# Online Assessment (OA) Guide - Sample Content

This is sample content for demonstrating the app. Replace it with your own notes and real interview experiences.

## Typical OA structure
Most campus online assessments have two or three sections. The first is an aptitude section covering quantitative ability, logical reasoning and verbal ability, usually 20 to 30 minutes. The second is a coding section with two or three problems, usually 60 to 90 minutes. Some companies add a CS fundamentals quiz covering operating systems, DBMS and computer networks.

## Coding section topics
Questions are mostly easy to medium difficulty. The most frequent topics are arrays and strings, hashing, two pointers, sliding window, binary search, basic dynamic programming, and trees and graphs (BFS and DFS). Expect one problem that needs an optimized solution under time limits, so brute force alone often fails the hidden test cases.

## Tips for the OA
Read all problems first and start with the one you are most confident about. Handle edge cases such as empty input, a single element, duplicates and very large values. Submit a working solution early, then optimize. Do not spend more than 25 minutes stuck on one problem.

## SQL and CS fundamentals
SQL questions focus on joins, GROUP BY with HAVING, and window functions such as ROW_NUMBER and RANK. For operating systems, revise process versus thread, deadlock conditions, scheduling algorithms and paging. For DBMS, revise normalization, ACID properties and indexing.
